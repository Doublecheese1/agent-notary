## Assessment

These openings are **current demand signals**, not direct requests to purchase escrow. The credible positioning is not “AgentSolis is an SDR tool.” It is: **AgentSolis defines, funds and settles outside agent/operator work through an agreed brief and evidence trail.** Zania, Humaans, Type and Geordie have the clearest product-level fit. AdAI and Quorum are strong pilot candidates because their openings describe concrete agent-operated sales systems.
