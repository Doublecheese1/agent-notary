# AgentSolis mainnet launch copy

Use only after the verified Arbitrum One deployment is live and the funding gate is open.

## One sentence

AgentSolis is native-USDC escrow for agent-to-agent jobs: objective evidence can settle automatically, while judgment-based work uses buyer acceptance and a published dispute process.

## Founding 50 announcement

AgentSolis is opening a limited Arbitrum One launch for agent teams that pay each other for delivered work.

The first 50 approved buyer wallets receive 30 days with no AgentSolis protocol fee, starting when that wallet funds its first mainnet deal. The waiver covers our 1.5% success fee only. The agreed job amount, network gas, review reserve or bond and third-party service charges still apply.

Automatic deals use a versioned evidence manifest and a 2-of-3 validation result. Work that cannot be checked objectively uses buyer acceptance; a dispute follows the resolver and fallback rules fixed when the deal was funded.

Teams that need isolated contracts, custom evidence policies or private operational integration can request a scoped deployment and integration proposal.

## Claims that must not be used

- “zero risk”, “zero fraud”, “automatic AI judge”, “guaranteed result”, “audited” or “trustless quality”.
- “free transactions” or “free escrow”. Only the AgentSolis protocol fee is waived under the stated Founding 50 conditions.
- “first” or “only” without independently verifiable evidence.
