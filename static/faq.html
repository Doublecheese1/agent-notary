BEFORE YOU SIGN

Common questions

Short answers for your first real AgentSolis job. The live contract, wallet confirmation and published terms govern your actual transaction.

Can I pay with a card or without a wallet?

No. The live flow requires a self-custody wallet on Arbitrum One. The wallet-free demo is only a simulation and moves no real USDC. AgentSolis does not offer hosted card checkout.

Does connecting MetaMask pay for a job?

No. MetaMask signs wallet transactions; connecting it does not transfer funds. MetaMask on desktop is currently tested and supported.

Which USDC do I need?

Circle native USDC on Arbitrum One, token address 0xaf88d065e77c8cC2239327C5EDb3A432268e5831. Do not use bridged USDC.e. Check the network and token address in your wallet.

Why do I also need ETH?

USDC is for the job and any applicable reserve or bond. A small amount of ETH on Arbitrum One pays network gas for wallet transactions. AgentSolis does not set that network fee.

What is the difference between Approve and Fund?

Approve gives the escrow contract permission to spend the exact displayed USDC amount; it does not transfer that USDC. Fund is the separate transaction that locks the approved USDC in escrow. Each transaction can need ETH for gas.

What changes in Buyer Review?

The buyer posts the job amount and the displayed buyer reserve. The seller posts a matching bond when accepting. A disputed delivery follows the published review and fallback process. Check the actual amounts and deadlines before signing.

How does Automatic differ from Buyer Review?

Automatic uses the agreed objective evidence rules and published fallback. Buyer Review lets the buyer accept or dispute delivery within the decision window; configured AI assistance and human review can be part of the dispute path. Neither mode guarantees a particular outcome.

Who can join Founding 50?

The first 50 approved buyer or seller participant wallets can receive a 30-day waiver of the AgentSolis protocol fee starting with that participant's first funded deal. Job payment, gas and applicable reserve or bond still apply. Read the live Founding 50 terms.

What if a physical item is involved?

Agree in advance on objective evidence such as a tracking number, delivery receipt, condition photos, an inspection window and a dispute deadline. A courier scan alone may not prove quality or acceptance. Define who reviews evidence and what happens if the parties disagree before funding.

Is the contract audited or publicly source verified?

Do not assume either. As of 29 October 2026, the live escrow source is not shown as verified by Sourcify. This is not an audit or safety guarantee. Review the risk disclosure and live configuration.
