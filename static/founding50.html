What you will test

The buyer creates a deal and shares one invitation link. The seller and arbitrator open that link with their designated wallets. If delivery is accepted, the seller receives the full test-USDC budget. If it is disputed, the agreed arbitrator can rule; the pre-agreed fallback applies only if the arbitrator does not rule in time.

Before you begin

The pilot checks your network, test ETH, test USDC and membership. It provides free faucet links when tokens are missing. MetaMask always asks before a transaction, and connecting a wallet alone never sends funds.
