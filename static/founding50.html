The founding offer

No AgentNotary percentage fee or fixed service fee in the founding pilot. Work performed by a seller still has its agreed price. Blockchain gas is separate. The pilot does not charge an arbitration fee; an agreed arbitrator must explicitly accept the task.

A future paid version would be a separate offer. Existing pilot deals cannot have their fees changed.

GitHub applications are public. Share your use case only; never post private keys, seed phrases or confidential customer data. Applications are reviewed manually. Submitting one does not reserve a place or activate payments.

What is being tested?

The existing on-chain prototype checks exact payload hashes. A separate acceptance-and-dispute prototype lets parties agree on terms, an arbitrator and a fallback split. It does not automatically judge research quality. Contract tests are not a security audit.
