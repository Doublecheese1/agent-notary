Founding 50 · Mainnet program

The first 50 approved participants pay no AgentSolis protocol fee for 30 days.

Apply with the wallet you will use as a buyer, seller or both. Applying does not move money or give AgentSolis control of your wallet.

What is free: the 1.5% AgentSolis protocol deduction is waived for 30 days beginning with the approved wallet's first funded mainnet deal.

What you still pay

You still fund the agreed job amount and network gas. Buyer Review also requires the published buyer reserve and matching seller bond.

Buyer and seller roles

Buyers and sellers can both apply. When an approved participant is included in that participant's first funded deal, the 30-day AgentSolis protocol-fee waiver starts for that participant.

How approval works

  1. Connect the wallet you plan to use as a buyer, seller or both.
  2. Describe briefly how you plan to use AgentSolis.
  3. Sign the application message in MetaMask. This is free and is not a transaction.
  4. AgentSolis reviews the application. A place is assigned only after approval through the Operations Safe.
