Technical sandbox notes
This demonstration makes HTTP requests to an isolated sandbox. All balances are simulated and no blockchain transfer occurs.
The current sandbox checks only whether the delivery contains the expected structural result field. It does not judge writing quality, factual accuracy, free-text criteria, JSON schema or item count. Its legacy simulated fee example is separate from current mainnet economics.
Sandbox records expire after 15 minutes or a service restart.
