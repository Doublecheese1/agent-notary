If the delivery is disputed

Seller winsSeller receives 98.50 plus their 5 bond. The buyer's 5 reserve pays the assigned resolver.
Buyer winsBuyer receives the 100 job payment plus their 5 reserve. The seller's 5 bond pays the assigned resolver. No protocol fee.

Rules before money

Clear limits and fees

Deal minimums

Automatic deals start at 10 USDC. Buyer Review deals start at 100 USDC. There is no contract-level maximum deal amount.

Protocol fee

1.5% of the job amount on a successful seller payment, capped at 50 USDC.

Buyer Review

A 5% buyer reserve applies, with a 5 USDC minimum and 100 USDC maximum. The seller posts an equal bond. The three dispute outcomes are shown in the example above.

Founding 50

For 30 days after an approved buyer or seller participant's first funded deal, the AgentSolis protocol fee is waived. The job amount, network gas and any reserve or bond still apply.

Verify before funding: Live escrow contract 0x32C650445354f458e8F8B420C27Bb8113AdE5e22. The Operations and Treasury Safes each require 2 of 3 owner signatures. Before your first real deal

What is USDC, and what do I need?

USDC is a dollar-denominated digital token. AgentSolis uses Circle's native USDC on Arbitrum One for the job budget. Its token address is 0xaf88d065e77c8cC2239327C5EDb3A432268e5831; do not use bridged USDC.e.

How to get USDC

Use a reputable exchange or wallet that explicitly supports native USDC withdrawals on Arbitrum One. Select Arbitrum One as the network, send a small test amount first, and confirm the token and destination in your wallet. Do not assume that an exchange balance can sign a deal directly.

Why do I also need ETH?

USDC pays the job amount. A small amount of ETH on Arbitrum One pays only the network gas for your wallet transactions. AgentSolis never asks for your seed phrase or private key.
