If the delivery is disputed

Seller winsSeller receives 98.50 plus their 5 bond. The buyer's 5 reserve pays the assigned resolver.
Buyer winsBuyer receives the 100 job payment plus their 5 reserve. The seller's 5 bond pays the assigned resolver. No protocol fee.
No ruling by the deadlineThe 100 job payment follows the agreed fallback split. Buyer reserve and seller bond return to their owners. No protocol fee.

Rules before money

Clear limits and fees

Every live-money condition stays visible before a wallet signature.

Funds are closed today. The interface is available for review and the wallet-free sandbox uses simulated balances. No public mainnet deposit is accepted until the verified contract, Safes, external review and internal canary checks are complete.

Deal minimums

Automatic deals start at 10 USDC. Buyer Review deals start at 100 USDC. There is no contract-level maximum deal amount.

Protocol fee

1.5% of the job amount on a successful seller payment, capped at 50 USDC.

Buyer Review

A 5% buyer reserve applies, with a 5 USDC minimum and 100 USDC maximum. The seller posts an equal bond. The three dispute outcomes are shown in the example above.

Founding 50

For 30 days after an approved buyer's first funded deal, the seller receives the job budget without the protocol deduction.
