Before you begin

These checks are a guide on this device; they do not inspect your wallet or save a readiness claim.

Try, then transact

  1. Try the wallet-free simulation; no real USDC moves.
  2. Choose Buyer or Seller in the transaction workspace.
  3. Buyer: define the work, approve the exact USDC allowance, then fund escrow in a separate wallet transaction.
  4. Seller: open the buyer's deal link, review the terms, and in Buyer Review approve and post the displayed bond before accepting.
  5. Save the deal link and follow the published delivery and review deadlines.

A connected wallet does not move money. Approval grants a limited USDC allowance; funding is the separate transfer into escrow. Your wallet shows the network fee before each signature. There is no card or wallet-free real-money checkout.
